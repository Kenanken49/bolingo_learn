# Bolingo Learn — User Guide

*Learn to code (and languages) by playing. This guide covers the app at
https://www.bolingo.co.ke and the iOS/Android builds — what everything does
and how to get the most out of it.*

## 1. Start here (5 minutes)

1. **Open the app or site.** No account needed to look around — guest mode
   plays lessons with local progress.
2. **Create an account** (Auth screen) when you want your progress saved,
   synced across devices, and backed up. Guests can't refill diamonds (there
   is no account to refill to), so sign in before you care about streaks.
3. **Pick your space.** Home → choose what you're learning (coding tracks,
   Swahili, English, Spanish, French, German). The path shows winding nodes:
   gold 🏆 done, pulsing = current, grey 🔒 locked (sequential once signed
   in).
4. **Play the pulsing node.** Tap tokens to build the answer, hit CHECK.
   Wrong answers are **always free** — experiment fearlessly.

## 2. The daily loop (the 80/20 of results)

1. **Clear today's leaf.** Seven leaves on the home trail light up with each
   streak day — today's unlit leaf is your reason to open the app.
2. **Do the current node + any due reviews.** Reviews (spaced repetition)
   count as much as new lessons for long-term retention.
3. **Claim unit chests.** Each chest pays bonus XP (30/50/75/100), gems, or a
   precious **streak freeze**. Open every chest you earn.
4. **Check Weekly Leagues** (Leaderboard). Leagues reset weekly; a little XP
   every day beats a weekend binge for promotion.

**Best-results recipe:** 1 node + reviews daily (≈15 min) > 10 nodes once a
week. Streaks reward showing up, not cramming.

## 3. Diamonds 💎 (your lesson attempts)

- You hold **5 max**. A **failed lesson** (under 80%) costs **1 diamond**.
  Wrong answers inside a lesson cost nothing.
- **Refill:** +1 every 4 hours automatically (timer shown in Refill), +1 per
  finished rewarded video, or unlimited with Premium.
- **At zero:** starting a lesson opens the refill sheet — watch, wait, or go
  Premium. Guests and Premium never lose diamonds.
- **Strategy:** attempt a lesson only when you can focus; use reviews (free
  practice) to warm up first. Never spend a video ad when the 4-hour timer is
  nearly done — let it tick.

## 4. Streaks 🔥, freezes 🛡️, XP ⭐

- **Streak** grows one day per active day. Miss a day and it resets —
  **unless a freeze covers it**. Each freeze covers exactly one missed day and
  is consumed doing so (Sidebar → *Streak shield n/5* explains this in-app).
- **Earn freezes** from unit chests. You can't buy them — open chests.
- **XP** levels you up; **gems** are the cosmetic/collectible currency shown
  in Settings. Combos (×2 banner) multiply lesson XP — accuracy streaks
  inside a lesson pay extra.

## 5. Paths, courses and exams

- **Coding tracks:** HTML, Python, Linux, chess-tactics-style logic and more —
  each unit runs **theory → workshop → solo lab → review → quiz → capstone
  project**. Build the project; it's the closest thing to the real job.
- **Languages (EN/ES/FR/DE/SW):** beginner survival topics, every exercise
  voiced by Livia (target language, slow) and Bingo (English around it).
  Tap **Say it back** and speak: green = exact, amber = accent-only, red =
  fix it. Bands: 90 nailed it, 75 close, 50 almost.
- **Pass mark is 80%.** Failed checks earn zero XP and become practice — the
  record counts first-try mastery, so treat the first attempt as the exam.

## 6. AI Sandbox: turn your own files into lessons 🧠

Home → **AI Sandbox FAB** → upload **text / PDF / photo of notes / audio /
video** (Premium needed for everything except small text files ≤1,000 words).

1. The pipeline detects what you uploaded: **books** become full narrated
   audiobooks; **notes/articles/transcripts** become a **revision pack**
   (recall, flashcards, chunking, mind map, memory palace, teach-it) and
   optionally quiz nodes.
2. Each finished upload appears as a **My Custom Revision** node on your
   path. In-flight uploads show as ⏳ with live status.
3. Choose your output in **Settings → Upload output**: both, audiobook-only
   (skips the curation cost), or quiz-nodes-only.
4. **Tips:** clean scans work best (good light, flat page); audio/video
   uploads ask a 4-point rights checklist first — only upload what you own or
   may use. Text under 1,000 words is free; a 500-page book bills past the
   monthly AI-word cap and will be rejected — split big books.

## 7. Audiobook Mode 🎧

Home → **Listen to Audiobook Mode**: your newest generated lesson narrates
segment by segment with the text highlighting karaoke-style. Tap any card to
jump; ⏮/⏭, play/pause, block counter included. Perfect for commutes —
generate the audiobook on Wi-Fi, then listen anywhere. 186 free classics live
in **Library → Audiobooks** too.

## 8. AI tutors: Bingo, Livia, Zora

- **Ask Bingo** (in-lesson): stuck on a question? Bingo gives a grounded
  hint, never the answer. Use it before burning attempts.
- **Roleplay** (`/roleplay`): pick Spanish/French/German, then **Livia**
  (patient mentor, one correction at a time) or **Bingo** (playful
  challenger). Reply in the target language; **Hear Livia/Bingo** replays the
  line. Code/career scenarios (Senior Dev Review, Debug Duck, Mock
  Interview) are below the language picker. Premium, stateless — each chat
  starts fresh.
- **Zora** is the build coach in projects; **Voice cast** in Settings lets
  you audition each character's voice per language before a lesson.

## 9. Labs, projects, certifications

- **Open Labs**: 1,800+ runnable exercises. JavaScript/CSS run on-device
  with per-test results; Git labs open a real terminal (clone/branch/merge…)
  graded on what you actually built; anything unrunnable ships a labeled
  **GUIDED** walkthrough instead — never a fake terminal.
- **Projects** end units with a live preview + test runner. Finish them:
  capstones unlock **Certifications**, each with a verifiable code you can
  share with employers (`/verify/[code]`).
- **Jobs** collects roles matched to your completed tracks.

## 10. Social: leagues, squads, rooms

- **Weekly Leagues** (Leaderboard): promotion/relegation by weekly XP.
- **Squad Cup**: fair team tournaments — same seed, same round for everyone.
- **Study rooms**: shared pomodoro timers with live presence. Body-doubling
  works; use it.
- **Friends & Community**: compare progress, find rooms and squads.

## 11. Library, Dictionary, Design, Avatar

- **Library**: 186 classic audiobooks + your generated books, your uploads,
  and the **Swahili↔English dictionary**.
- **Design lab**: a full UI/UX track (hierarchy, scaling, user-centered
  labs, capstone redesign) for designers.
- **Avatar/Life stages**: your visual learner identity; **Explore** searches
  all content; **My learning** resumes exactly where you stopped.

## 12. Settings tour (worth 2 minutes)

Profile & streak stats · Voice cast auditions · Upload output preference ·
Appearance (jungle dark / quantum-paper light) · App language · Reminders
(daily nudge, survives restarts) · Anonymous diagnostics opt-out (clears the
queue instantly) · Ad privacy options · **Offline AI & sync** (download the
on-device model, prefer-offline tutor, knowledge stats) · Quality console
(owner only).

## 13. Premium 👑 (KSh 770/month) & payments

Premium = unlimited diamonds, PDF/scan/AV uploads, smart AI curation, elite
voices, 100k AI words/month, no ads. Pay via **Google Play / App Store**
(RevenueCat, cancel in store settings) or **M-Pesa STK push on Android**
(when released in your region — you approve on your phone; price is set by
the server, never typed by you). Restore purchases any time from the paywall.
Free forever tier: starter lessons, ≤1,000-word text uploads, device-voice
narration.

## 14. Web vs mobile

Same account, same progress. **Mobile app** is best for speaking practice
(mic), audiobooks on the go, and reminders. **Web** is best for labs,
reading, and big screens. Download the **on-device AI model** (Offline AI
screen) before a flight and the tutor + knowledge search keep working with no
connection; progress syncs when you're back.

## 15. Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Lesson won't start (refill sheet) | 0 diamonds: watch a video, wait for the timer, or go Premium |
| No sound / wrong language voice | Settings → Voice cast: audition, install the missing voice it names |
| Say-it-back missing | Needs mic + OS speech recognition (dev build on native; Chrome/Safari on web) |
| Upload stuck at ⏳ | Check size/type vs your tier; audio/video need the rights checklist; retry — transcripts are cached so retries don't double-bill |
| Progress lost as guest | Sign in — guest progress lives on the device only |
| Update not appearing | Pull/restart — OTA covers JS instantly; native changes need a store update |
| Ads feel wrong | Settings → ad privacy; Premium removes them entirely |

Data & privacy in one line: your files and progress are private to your
account, speech audio is deleted after scoring, ads need consent, diagnostics
are anonymous and optional — full text in `privacy-policy.md`.

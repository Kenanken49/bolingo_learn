The adaptive principle

Same moment: you just finished a lesson.

One completion event, three completely different payoffs. The data underneath is identical; the psychology on top is not.

Why people come back

Three reward loops, tuned to three brains.

Explorer loop

  1. TriggerSquad Cup starts Friday; Bingo: "two nodes to the boss"
  2. ActionA 3-minute node with a combo meter
  3. RewardBoss hits, XP, a guaranteed cosmetic chest
  4. InvestmentAvatar unlocks and squad standing

Launcher loop

  1. Trigger"14 studying now" in your goal's room
  2. ActionA 25-minute focus block with peers
  3. RewardPath percentage and job match rise
  4. InvestmentPortfolio projects and verified skills

Climber loop

  1. TriggerThe commute itself; downloaded audio is waiting
  2. ActionPress play once
  3. RewardFinish date moves closer; time feels well spent
  4. InvestmentCertificate progress and a weekly plan

Engaging, not exploitative

The dopamine has rules.

Variable rewards and streaks are powerful, and a third of these learners are children. These limits are part of the design, not an afterthought.

Explorer (10–18)

  • Chests always pay out, prizes are cosmetic, and no chest, gem or heart can be bought with money.
  • A daily play cap (default 60 min) that ends with a "great session" screen, not a cliffhanger.
  • Quiet hours 21:00–06:30: no notifications, no streak risk.
  • Streak shields are earned automatically each week; a missed day is never shown as failure.
  • Tournaments are class or invite-code only, with no open chat.
  • A weekly summary for a parent or teacher, if one is linked.

Launcher (19–25)

  • Job-match scores use only proven skills, never paid boosts that hide gaps.
  • Rooms are camera-optional, with one-tap report and block.
  • No leaderboards of salaries or hours. Progress compares you to your own goal.

Climber (26–40)

  • Consistency is counted per month, so one busy day never resets anything.
  • At most one notification a day, sent at the commute time the learner picked.
  • Every celebration can be turned off in one switch.

Today vs. design target. Built in the app: the age dial and stage hubs, Squad Cup live team quizzes (private squad codes, no chat), the 3D-tilt avatar studio with earned unlocks, live study rooms (shared focus timer, fixed statuses and reactions, no free text), the job marketplace (admin-posted roles matched to earned certifications) and public credential links at bolingo.co.ke/verify/<code>, plus the offline tutor, Smart Sync, certificates, audiobooks and roleplay. Still design targets: the quest-map chapter art, boss battles inside lessons, chests in Squad Cup, true 3D avatar models, camera video in rooms, daily play caps and quiet hours, parent summaries, and the Career Fast-Pass.
